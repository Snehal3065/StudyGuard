# StudyGuard Laptop & Browser Guard (Chrome / Edge / Brave / Opera)

This extension brings StudyGuard's distraction protection to your laptop browser (Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebook).

### ✨ Key Features:
1. **Study Avatar Profile & Identity**:
   - Features the StudyGuard Focus Cadet avatar right in the extension popup and on YouTube.
   - Live status indicator and deep focus mode tracker.

2. **Strict YouTube Distraction Blocker (Subscriptions & Recommendations Locked)**:
   - **Subscriptions Tab & List Blocked**: Hides the Subscriptions button in the sidebar and channel lists. Direct visits to `/feed/subscriptions` are blocked.
   - **Home Page Algorithmic Recommendations Blocked**: The infinite recommendation feed on YouTube Home is replaced by a clean, calm StudyGuard Focus Dashboard.
   - **Only Intentional Study Permitted**: Clean Home and direct keyword searching (e.g. searching for tutorials or lecture topics) remain accessible.
   - **Sidebar & Video Distractions Removed**: Hides related/recommended videos sidebar, end-screen cards, and comment rabbit holes on video pages.
   - **YouTube Shorts Blocked**: Removes all Shorts buttons, shelves, and redirects `/shorts/` URLs.

3. **🎓 YouTube Study Marathon (45m Study / 15m Break Mode)**:
   - Paste any YouTube lecture or tutorial link (even 3-hour or 5-hour long marathons).
   - **Strict Lockdown**: Locks your browser exclusively to that single lecture video. All other YouTube videos, tabs, and feeds are blocked!
   - **Automated 45m / 15m Intervals**:
     - 45 minutes of pure uninterrupted study with live floating HUD timer.
     - Automatic video pause when the 45-minute sprint ends.
     - Scheduled 15-minute full-screen break screen with soothing chime alerts to hydrate and stretch.
     - Auto-resumes video when the 15-minute break ends for the next sprint!
     - Sprints continue automatically until the video finishes!

4. **Multi-Platform Distraction Shield**:
   - Blocks Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Twitter/X, Netflix, Twitch, Discord, and Facebook during study mode.

5. **🎵 Offline Study Music & Audio Studio**:
   - **Built-in Offline Synthesizers**: Binaural Alpha Waves (432Hz / 10Hz differential), Deep Study Rain & Thunder, White Noise Flow State, Lo-Fi Focus Chamber, Nature Stream, Classical Piano. Generated 100% offline via Web Audio API.
   - **Audio Importer**: Import your own local MP3, WAV, or M4A study files.
   - **Playlists**: Create custom study playlists and play them continuously.
   - **Pop Out Fullscreen Studio**: Click "Pop Out ↗️" to open the dedicated player tab (`music.html`) so study beats play continuously in the background while you work!

6. **🔄 Cross-Device Sync Hub**:
   - View combined focus stats: Phone Study Time + Laptop Study Time = Combined Total Focus.
   - Tracks study marathon time on laptop and exports stats for instant pairing with the StudyGuard Android app.
   - 1-click JSON export/import and pairing code support.

### 🚀 How to Install on Your Laptop (Takes 30 seconds):
1. On your laptop, open Google Chrome, Brave, or Microsoft Edge.
2. In the address bar, go to: `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`).
3. In the top-right corner, turn ON the switch for **"Developer mode"**.
4. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select this `laptop-extension` folder.
6. Click the Extensions puzzle icon 🧩 in your browser toolbar and pin **StudyGuard** 🛡️.
